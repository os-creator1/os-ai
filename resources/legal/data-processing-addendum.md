## 8. DPIA, Article 32–36 and regulatory assistance

Company shall assist Customer, taking account of the nature of processing and information available, with security obligations, breach notifications, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with supervisory authorities where required by Articles 32–36. Any lawful compensation for extraordinary bespoke assistance shall not undermine mandatory assistance duties.

## 9. International transfers

9.1. Company shall not transfer Customer Personal Data in violation of applicable restrictions. Where lawful transfer requires safeguards, the parties shall adopt and complete relevant EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), adequacy mechanisms, or another valid legal instrument and supplementary measures as necessary.

9.2. The parties shall verify correct SCC modules, roles, destination, governing clauses, competent authorities and annexes for each restricted transfer. The official SCC text must be incorporated **unaltered except as allowed**; this DPA is not itself a substitute.

9.3. UK and Swiss transfers may require separate addenda or instruments. Annex D records verified transfer arrangements. If legally binding SCCs conflict with these commercial terms, the SCCs prevail to the extent required.

## 10. Data deletion and return

10.1. At the end of processing, at Customer's choice and subject to Article 28(3)(g), Company shall **delete or return** Customer Personal Data and delete existing copies unless applicable Union/Member State law requires retention.

10.2. The operational procedure, export method, backup handling, deletion windows and lawful exceptions must be accurately set out in Annex B or an incorporated retention schedule. No promise of immediate deletion, universal data portability or unlimited archiving is made absent actual support.

10.3. Data lawfully retained after termination remains protected and may be used only for the retention's lawful purpose.

## 11. Information, audit and inspection

11.1. Company shall make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 obligations and allow and contribute to audits and inspections conducted by Customer or a qualified auditor mandated by Customer, as legally required.

11.2. Parties may coordinate advance notice, confidentiality, security, access to other tenants' data, time, evidence, independent reports and scope to protect systems and persons. Reasonable arrangements may not negate statutory audit rights.

11.3. Customer shall not use an audit to access unrelated customer data, security secrets or proprietary material beyond what applicable law requires.

## 12. Controller duties

Customer is responsible for ensuring a valid processing legal basis, relevant notices, consent where applicable, proper instructions, data minimization, necessary DPIAs, lawful transfers under its control, retention instructions and processing purposes.

Customer must not direct Company to process unlawful material. Customer will select features and configure permissions appropriately. Its responsibilities do not release Company from processor-specific duties.

## 13. Processing of special categories

The platform should not be assumed suitable for unrestricted special-category or highly regulated data. Customer must identify any such processing, establish Article 9 conditions where relevant and determine with Company whether supported safeguards and contract terms are sufficient. We may refuse unsupported processing.

## 14. Cooperation and authorities

Parties shall cooperate in good faith on lawful requests, supervisory inquiries, relevant access requests and investigations to the extent required by their respective legal roles. Company may challenge or narrow overbroad requests where legally appropriate and possible.

## 15. Liability and conflicting agreements

Commercial indemnities and liability provisions of the Terms apply only where lawful. Neither party may use this DPA to exclude GDPR duties or remedies that cannot lawfully be limited, change statutory joint or several liability to injured individuals, or defeat official SCC obligations.

## 16. Duration and survival

The DPA applies while Company processes Customer Personal Data and continues for any surviving lawful retention, deletion, audit, confidentiality or other legally required obligations.
